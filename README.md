# Walmart Company Analysis 2023–2024

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents a financial and strategic analysis of **Walmart Inc.** for the fiscal years 2023–2024. It covers Walmart’s business segments, current activities, future prospects, financial statements, ratio analysis, SWOT analysis, and overall company performance.

The analysis highlights Walmart’s strong revenue growth, investments in technology and automation, expanding e-commerce presence, and continued focus on cost leadership and operational efficiency.

## 🏢 About Walmart

Walmart Inc. was founded in 1962 by Sam Walton in Rogers, Arkansas, USA. The company is headquartered in Bentonville, Arkansas, and is one of the world’s largest retailers by revenue. Walmart operates through three major segments:

- Walmart U.S.
- Walmart International
- Sam’s Club

Walmart’s mission is to help people **“save money, live better.”**

## 📊 Key Financial Highlights

- FY2024 total revenue: approximately **$648.1 billion**
- FY2024 global net sales: approximately **$642.6 billion**
- Walmart U.S. net sales: **$441.8 billion**
- Walmart International net sales: **$114.6 billion**
- Sam’s Club net sales: **$86.2 billion**
- Net income increased significantly in FY2024 compared to FY2023
- Operating cash flow improved, showing stronger operational efficiency

## 🔍 SWOT Analysis

### Strengths
- Strong global brand reputation
- Large-scale operations
- Stable revenue growth
- Efficient supply chain
- Growing e-commerce presence

### Weaknesses
- Heavy reliance on the U.S. market
- Labor-related challenges
- Strong competition in e-commerce
- Slower international growth

### Opportunities
- Expansion in emerging markets
- Growth in technology and AI
- Sustainability initiatives
- Drone delivery and supply chain automation
- Private label brand expansion

### Threats
- Intense competition from Amazon, Costco, and Target
- Inflation and economic uncertainty
- Changing consumer behavior
- Regulatory challenges
- Technology disruption

## 🚀 Current and Future Initiatives
